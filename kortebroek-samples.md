# Shorts Appropriateness Examples

## Example 1: Casual Friday Office Attire
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 25°C
2. Weather: Sunny
3. Occasion: Work
4. Dress Code: Casual Friday
5. Time: Noon (working hours)

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are appropriate, specifically neat bermuda or chino style shorts paired with a professional top and proper footwear.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A professional-looking person wearing neat, khaki bermuda shorts with a light blue button-down shirt at an office on Casual Friday. The setting shows a bright, sunny day visible through large windows, with the clock showing noon. The atmosphere is professional yet relaxed, and other colleagues in the background are dressed in various casual Friday attire. The temperature indicator in the scene shows 25°C.
```

## Example 5: Outdoor Business Networking Event
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 38°C
2. Weather: Extremely hot, sunny day
3. Occasion: Professional networking event
4. Dress Code: Business casual (modified for extreme heat)
5. Time: Evening, 2-hour outdoor garden reception

**Recommendation:**
Due to the extreme temperature, tailored dress shorts are appropriate in this setting despite the business casual nature. Opt for high-quality, knee-length dress shorts in neutral colors paired with a pressed short-sleeve button-down shirt or a quality polo shirt. Proper leather loafers or boat shoes (not sandals) complete the look. This balances professionalism with practical comfort in extreme heat.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown

```

## Example 6: Spring Day in the Netherlands
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 15°C
2. Weather: Clear with minimal precipitation chance (2%)
3. Occasion: General everyday activity
4. Dress Code: None specified (casual)
5. Time: Daytime, May 22, 2025

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are generally not recommended at this temperature. At 15°C (59°F), it's relatively cool for most people to comfortably wear shorts. This aligns with the Dutch website "Kan ik een korte broek aan?" recommendation of "nee" (no) for these conditions.

For most people, this temperature would be more comfortable with long pants or light trousers. If you are particularly active (like running, hiking, or playing sports) or are not sensitive to cooler temperatures, you might still find shorts acceptable, but for general wear, longer bottoms would be more appropriate.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A spring day in the Netherlands with a temperature display showing 15°C and mostly clear skies. People are walking along a canal wearing light jackets, long pants, and occasionally light sweaters. A digital calendar showing May 22, 2025 is visible in the scene. The atmosphere is pleasant but slightly cool, with trees in full spring foliage and some flowers blooming. A small weather station shows minimal chance of rain at 2%. A few active joggers in the background are wearing shorts, but most pedestrians have opted for long pants.
```

2. Weather: Partly cloudy with occasional sunshine
3. Occasion: King's Day celebration (national holiday)

## Example 8: Dutch North Sea Beach in Autumn
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 12°C
2. Weather: Windy with dramatic clouds, occasional sunbreaks
3. Occasion: Weekend beach walk in Scheveningen
4. Dress Code: Casual outdoor
5. Time: October afternoon, 2-hour beach walk

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are not appropriate for this scenario. The combination of 12°C temperature, autumn winds from the North Sea, and the exposed beach environment would make shorts uncomfortable and impractical. Opt for long pants, preferably windproof, with layers on top including a windbreaker jacket. The "Kan ik een korte broek aan?" recommendation would be a definite "nee" for these conditions.

```markdown

People are walking along the shoreline bundled in windbreaker jackets and long pants, with some wearing scarves. The North Sea appears choppy with white-capped waves, and the famous Scheveningen Pier is visible in the background. Beach cafés have their outdoor heaters turned on, and some people are enjoying hot drinks at covered terraces. A weather station near the beachfront promenade shows 12°C, and flags are flapping vigorously in the wind. Seagulls hover above as beachcombers search for shells, all dressed appropriately for the cool, windy conditions.
```

## Example 9: Keukenhof Gardens in Tulip Season
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 16°C
2. Weather: Sunny with light breeze
3. Occasion: Tourist visit to Keukenhof Gardens
4. Dress Code: Casual tourist attire
5. Time: Mid-April, full-day garden excursion

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are marginally appropriate but not ideal. At 16°C with sunshine, some people might find shorts comfortable, especially when walking actively through the extensive gardens. However, most visitors would be more comfortable in light long pants or perhaps convertible hiking pants that offer the option to zip off into shorts if it becomes warmer during midday. Consider individual temperature sensitivity and activity level - photographers who may spend time kneeling or sitting to capture tulips would benefit from long pants.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A sunny spring day at Keukenhof Gardens in the Netherlands with vibrant tulip displays in peak bloom. Tourists are exploring the colorful flower beds and ornamental gardens, most wearing light long pants or jeans with light sweaters or long-sleeve shirts, while a few are in shorts. The windmill landmark of Keukenhof is visible in the background against a bright blue sky with a few puffy clouds. Digital signs throughout the garden display 16°C, and the light breeze is visible as it gently moves the tulips. Tour groups with cameras are taking photos, and cafés with outdoor seating are busy with visitors enjoying the pleasant but not overly warm spring weather.
```

## Example 10: Rotterdam Winter Market
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 3°C
2. Weather: Clear winter evening with frost forming
3. Occasion: Evening visit to winter/Christmas market
4. Dress Code: Winter casual
5. Time: December evening, 3-hour visit

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are entirely inappropriate for this scenario. The cold winter temperature of 3°C with evening frost requires proper winter attire including long, warm pants, winter coat, scarf, hat, and gloves. Choosing shorts in these conditions would not only be uncomfortable but potentially dangerous from a health perspective, risking hypothermia with prolonged exposure.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A winter evening at the Rotterdam Christmas market with stalls illuminated by festive lights and decorations. People are bundled up in winter coats, scarves, hats, and gloves, with visible breath vapor in the cold air. Market stalls are selling hot food, glühwein, and holiday crafts, creating a warm atmosphere despite the cold. The Markthal and Cube Houses are visible in the background, their distinctive architecture highlighted by seasonal lighting. Digital temperature displays show 3°C, and frost is beginning to form on railings and unused benches. Visitors are walking on slightly frosty pathways between stalls, clearly dressed for proper winter conditions with multiple warm layers and absolutely no shorts in sight.
```

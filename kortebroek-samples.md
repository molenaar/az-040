# Shorts Appropriateness Examples

## Example 1: Casual Friday Office Attire
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 25°C
2. Weather: Sunny
3. Occasion: Work
4. Dress Code: Casual Friday
5. Time: Noon (working hours)

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are appropriate, specifically neat bermuda or chino style shorts paired with a professional top and proper footwear.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A professional-looking person wearing neat, khaki bermuda shorts with a light blue button-down shirt at an office on Casual Friday. The setting shows a bright, sunny day visible through large windows, with the clock showing noon. The atmosphere is professional yet relaxed, and other colleagues in the background are dressed in various casual Friday attire. The temperature indicator in the scene shows 25°C.
```

## Example 2: Outdoor Sports Event
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 30°C
2. Weather: Sunny with high humidity
3. Occasion: Attending a baseball game
4. Dress Code: None specified (casual spectator)
5. Time: Afternoon, 3-hour event

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are highly appropriate due to the hot temperature, outdoor setting, and casual nature of the event. Lightweight, breathable materials are recommended.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A person wearing comfortable cargo shorts and a breathable t-shirt with a baseball cap for sun protection, sitting in stadium seating at a baseball game. The scene shows a bright, sunny day with visible heat waves, and people using fans to cool themselves. The temperature display on the scoreboard shows 30°C, and spectators are dressed in casual, cool attire enjoying cold drinks and ice cream.
```

## Example 3: Business Networking Event
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 22°C
2. Weather: Indoor climate-controlled environment
3. Occasion: Professional networking event
4. Dress Code: Business casual
5. Time: Evening, 2-hour cocktail reception

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are not appropriate for this setting. Despite comfortable temperatures, the business casual dress code and professional nature of the event make long pants or a skirt more suitable.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A diverse group of professionals mingling at an elegant evening networking reception in a climate-controlled hotel conference space. Attendees are wearing business casual attire - men in dress pants with button-down shirts or polo shirts, women in slacks, skirts, or dresses. The ambiance features soft lighting, standing cocktail tables with appetizers, and a digital thermostat on the wall showing 22°C. The atmosphere is professional yet relaxed with people exchanging business cards.
```

## Example 4: Beach Vacation
**Input Parameters:**
1. Temperature: 32°C
2. Weather: Sunny with occasional breeze
3. Occasion: Beach vacation/leisure
4. Dress Code: None (vacation attire)
5. Time: All day activity

**Recommendation:**
Shorts are highly appropriate and recommended. Consider swimwear, beach shorts, or light breathable shorts with UV protection.

**DALL-E Prompt:**
```markdown
A person enjoying a beach vacation wearing comfortable linen shorts and a light, loose-fitting shirt, standing on golden sand with the ocean in the background. The scene captures a bright sunny day with a few fluffy clouds, palm trees swaying in a gentle breeze, and beachgoers with sunhats, sunglasses and beach umbrellas. A nearby weather station display shows 32°C, and the person is applying sunscreen while looking relaxed and happy in the vacation setting.
```
